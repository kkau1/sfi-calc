Module 01 • Pressure Booster System

Fixed Speed Vessel Sizing (定速泵壓力鼓容積計算)

Governing Formulas (計算公式)
V = (16.5 × Qmax × Po(abs) × Pi(abs)) / [a × (Po - Pi) × Pp(abs)]
ΔV (Usable 有效儲水量) = V × Pp(abs) × [1/Pi(abs) - 1/Po(abs)]
* Where Qmax is converted to L/min and P(abs) = P(gauge) + 1.0 bar

• Pp (Pre-charge): Typically set to 0.9 × Pi so diaphragm does not bottom out.

• Starts/hr (a): Smaller pumps (<4 kW) allow 20–30 starts/hr; larger pumps require 10–15 starts/hr.

Module 03 • Electrical & Mechanical Sizing

Pump Shaft Power & Motor Selection (水泵軸功率及摩打計算)

Governing Formula (計算公式)
P2 (Shaft Power) = (Q × H × SG) / (367 × η)
* Where Q is in m³/h, H is in meters (m), SG is Specific Gravity (1.0 for water), and η is pump efficiency (0–1)

BS 5306-2:1990 Formulas (計算公式)
Loss (Px) = P1 - P2
≤ 65mm Pipe: Po = Px × (500 / Q)2
> 65mm Pipe: Po = Px × (5000 / Q)2
K Factor = Q / √Px (where Q is in L/min and Px is in Bar)

Module 06 • General Hydraulic Restriction

General Orifice Plate Sizing (通用減壓孔板計算)

Note: The simplified calculation applies to orifices where the orifice diameter is less than 0.3 of the pipe size (d/D < 0.3).
Governing Formula (計算公式)
Q = Cd × A × √(2ΔP / ρ)
* Where ρ = 1000 kg/m³ (Water), A = π × (d/2)², and Cd is Discharge Coefficient

* Default 0.62 for sharp-edged orifice

* To verify d/D < 0.3 β-ratio rule

Enter flow rate, pressure drop, and Cd to calculate required orifice diameter.
